# Backlink Kit — Lucoze off-page engine

Backlinks are the biggest off-site ranking lever and they're **user-owned** (outreach). This is
the playbook: who to contact, what to send, and what *not* to do. Work top-down — Tier 1 is
fastest, Tier 2–3 compound. Track everything in one sheet (columns at the bottom).

**Golden rules:** quality ≫ quantity. **Never** buy links, use PBNs, or spam directories — Google's
link-spam systems neutralize or penalize them. One link from ETHealthworld beats 100 from junk.

---

## Tier 1 — SaaS/software directories ⏱️ fastest wins (do first)
Dual benefit: a **backlink** + you appear in the "best HMS India" listicles that own the head terms
you can't rank for yet. Claim/create a listing, paste from [`listing-pack.md`](listing-pack.md), link
back to `https://lucoze.com/in/`, then ask design-partner clinics for reviews.

Priority (many started in Phase 0 — finish the set):
- **G2** · **Capterra** · **GetApp** · **Software Advice** (Gartner network — one submission often feeds all)
- **SoftwareSuggest** · **TechJockey** · **Techimply** · **TechnologyCounter** (India-focused, rank well here)
- **GoodFirms** · **Crozdesk** · **SaaSworthy** · **SourceForge / Slashdot**
- Healthcare-specific: **Software Finder / Healthcare IT directories**, **Ambest/medical-software lists** where relevant

## Tier 2 — India healthtech media (digital PR) — highest authority
Earned coverage from these passes strong authority + brand mentions (which also help AI/LLM
citation). You write well — repurpose the blog into pitched op-eds.
- **YourStory**, **Inc42**, **ETHealthworld** (Economic Times), **Express Healthcare**, **BW Healthcare World**,
  **Healthcare Executive**, **Medical Buyer**, **Health Analytics India / AIM**
- **Angles that land** (tie to your real strengths):
  1. *ABDM/ABHA explained for clinic owners* — you already have the depth (link your ABDM cluster).
  2. *DPDP 2023 for small clinics* — a compliance explainer; timely and under-covered.
  3. *Founder story* — "built in Konnagar for East-India clinics"; the design-partner program; why data-in-India.
  4. *The nursing-home software gap in East India* — a genuinely under-served niche angle.

## Tier 3 — Partnerships & local (East India) — durable + on-brand
- **IMA (Indian Medical Association)** state/district chapters (esp. West Bengal, Bihar, Odisha) — newsletters, member resources.
- **Lab & pharma distributors**, medical-equipment suppliers, **CA/accounting firms** serving clinics — mutual referrals + links.
- **WB/East-India startup ecosystem**: Webel, Startup Bengal, NASSCOM East, local incubators — startup directories + press.
- Local healthcare associations, nursing-home associations (WB has active ones).

## Tier 4 — Expert quotes (HARO-style) — steady, low-effort
Sign up and answer journalist requests as an **ABDM/DPDP/clinic-operations expert**:
- **Featured** (featured.com, formerly HARO) · **Qwoted** · **SourceBottle** · **Help a B2B Writer**
- Set alerts for: ABDM, ABHA, DPDP, healthtech India, clinic software, EMR, hospital management.
