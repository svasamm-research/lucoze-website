<h2 id="what">What is DHIS?</h2>
<p>
	<strong>DHIS</strong> is the <strong>Digital Health Incentive Scheme</strong>, run by the National
	Health Authority (NHA) under the{" "}
	<a href="/in/blog/abdm-abha-guide/">Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)</a>. The idea is
	simple: rather than only requiring hospitals to go digital, the NHA pays a financial incentive for
	the digital activity that ABDM wants — creating and linking records to patients' ABHA accounts.
</p>
<p>
	So the same work that makes you ABDM-active — issuing an ABHA-linked discharge summary,
	prescription or report — can also earn an incentive, rather than just being an overhead.
</p>

<h2 id="how">How the incentive works, in shape</h2>
<p>
	The exact rates and caps are set by NHA notification and have been revised over time, so treat the
	<em>mechanism</em> as the durable part and the numbers as "check the portal":
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		The incentive is paid per <strong>qualifying ABDM transaction</strong> — broadly, ABHA-linked
		records created and shared with patient consent.
	</li>
	<li>
		It typically kicks in <strong>above a monthly threshold</strong> of such records, and is{" "}
		<strong>capped</strong> — so it rewards real, ongoing volume, not a one-off.
	</li>
	<li>
		It runs through your facility's ABDM identity, which is why{" "}
		<a href="/in/blog/hfr-explained/">being on the HFR</a> and having your doctors on the{" "}
		<a href="/in/blog/register-doctors-on-hpr/">HPR</a> matter — no verified facility and
		clinicians, no qualifying records.
	</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="eligible">How do you become eligible?</h2>
<p>
	There's no shortcut around the basics — the incentive follows the same rails as the rest of ABDM:
</p>
<ul>
	<li>
		Register the facility on the <a href="/in/blog/hfr-explained/">HFR</a> and your clinicians on
		the HPR.
	</li>
	<li>
		Actually create and link ABHA records in daily work — the{" "}
		<a href="/in/blog/abha-first-visit-front-desk/">front desk creating an ABHA at first visit</a>{" "}
		is where this begins.
	</li>
	<li>Enrol for the incentive on the NHA/ABDM portal and meet the current scheme conditions.</li>
</ul>
<p>
	The whole path — HFR, HPR, ABHA at the desk — is laid out in{" "}
	<a href="/in/blog/abdm-onboarding-for-providers/">the ABDM onboarding process for a provider</a>.
</p>

<h2 id="why">Why it's worth doing properly</h2>
<p>
	Two reasons. First, ABDM participation is drifting from optional toward expected, so you'll likely
	do the work anyway — the incentive just makes it pay for part of itself. Second, the hospitals
	that earn it are the ones creating records <em>as part of the normal workflow</em>, not as a
	separate chore someone remembers at month-end. That only happens if your system issues ABHA-linked
	records by default.
</p>
