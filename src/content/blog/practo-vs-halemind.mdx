<h2 id="approach">How did we approach this?</h2>
<p>
	This is a comparison written by a competitor. We are not going to pretend it is objective. Read it
	as: "here is what we believe about three products we know in detail, based on hands-on evaluation
	and conversations with clinic owners using each."
</p>
<p>
	We are still in design-partner phase — our customer reference list is small and growing. Where a
	claim below would benefit from a customer reference we don't yet have, we say so explicitly. We'd
	rather you trust this than spin synthetic case studies.
</p>

<h2 id="practo">Practo Ray</h2>
<p>
	<strong>Best for:</strong> solo practitioners and small dental practices where the patient-flow
	already runs on Practo's discovery network. Strong integration with the Practo consumer app means
	appointments flow in via Practo's marketplace.
</p>
<p>
	<strong>Where it strains:</strong> multi-doctor clinics. Billing across more than 2-3 doctors gets
	clumsy. GST handling is bolted on. Inventory and pharmacy are not native. ABDM integration is
	partial. For the feature-by-feature view, see our <a href="/in/compare/practo-ray/">Lucoze vs
	Practo Ray comparison</a>.
</p>

<h2 id="halemind">Halemind</h2>
<p>
	<strong>Best for:</strong> small hospitals (20-60 beds) with established back-office processes.
	The IPD module is mature. NABH-oriented reporting is solid.
</p>
<p>
	<strong>Where it strains:</strong> the UX is dated. Hindi UI is partial. Onboarding takes 4-8
	weeks of administrator time. Pricing is per-module and adds up faster than the brochure suggests. See the full <a href="/in/compare/halemind/">Lucoze vs Halemind comparison</a> for the row-by-row view.
</p>

<h2 id="lucoze">Lucoze</h2>
<p>
	<strong>Best for:</strong> 3-15 doctor clinics, multi-specialty practices, and small hospitals in
	East India — places where Hindi-first UI, India-hosted data, and design-partner-style support
	matter more than legacy module breadth.
</p>
<p>
	<strong>Where we're honest about being behind:</strong> we don't have 5 years of hospital
	references. Our IPD module is younger than Halemind's. Our Practo-style consumer marketplace
	simply doesn't exist — we route bookings, we don't generate them.
</p>

<h2 id="pick">How do you pick?</h2>
<ul>
	<li>
		<strong>Solo dentist or single-doctor clinic that already gets bookings from Practo?</strong>{" "}
		Practo Ray. Don't overthink it.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>
			40-bed hospital with an established administrator who values broad legacy modules?
		</strong>{" "}
		Halemind, if the budget supports it.
	</li>
	<li>
		<strong>
			Multi-doctor East-India clinic or small hospital that wants Hindi-first, India-hosted, and a
			founder you can WhatsApp?
		</strong>{" "}
		Lucoze. That's the deal we built for.
	</li>
</ul>
