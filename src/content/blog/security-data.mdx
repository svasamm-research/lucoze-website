<h2 id="commitment">The commitment we've made</h2>
<p>
	Before our first paying customer signs, here's a commitment we want on the record: every byte of
	paying-customer patient data will sit in India — AWS Mumbai (ap-south-1) or DigitalOcean Bangalore
	(BLR1). Not Virginia. Not Singapore. Not some "global" region that resolves to wherever the
	provider felt like.
</p>
<p>
	India hosting costs noticeably more than US/EU equivalents — typically 30–40% more depending on
	the workload. We accept that cost as table stakes for the customer relationship we want.
</p>
<p>
	<strong>Honest disclosure about today.</strong> While we're in design-partner phase with synthetic
	test data, our infrastructure runs on Hetzner (EU) for cost reasons. The instant a real patient
	record enters the system, the production host moves to ap-south-1 or BLR1 — that's a contractual
	line, not a roadmap item.
</p>

<h2 id="dpdp">The legal reason</h2>
<p>
	The DPDP Act 2023 leaves cross-border data transfer as a footnote you don't want to be reading in
	2027 with 80 clinics on your platform and a regulator's notice on your desk. The exact rules are
	still being clarified. The safe call — the one a clinic owner doesn't need a lawyer to verify —
	is: data stays in India.
</p>

<h2 id="practical">The practical reason</h2>
<p>
	Round-trip latency from a Tier-2 clinic in Patna to Mumbai is ~40ms. To us-east, it's ~280ms. For
	a front-desk staffer searching for a patient 200 times a day, that 240ms difference is the gap
	between software that feels like a tool and software that feels like a tax.
</p>

<h2 id="trust">The trust reason</h2>
<p>
	The buyer question we can't dodge: "Where does our patient data live?" The answer that costs us
	trust — with the buyer, with the regulator, eventually with the regulator's notice — is anywhere
	outside India.
</p>

<h2 id="what">What production will look like</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Primary database: AWS RDS (ap-south-1) or DO Managed Postgres (BLR1).</li>
	<li>Object storage: AWS S3 (ap-south-1) or DO Spaces (BLR1).</li>
	<li>Backups: same region + read-replica in another Indian AZ. Never cross-region.</li>
	<li>Encryption: at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).</li>
	<li>Key management: customer-managed keys per tenant.</li>
	<li>Audit log: every record access logged for 7 years.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="three-questions">Three questions to ask any vendor</h2>
<ol>
	<li>Where is the primary patient database hosted? Not the CDN. The database.</li>
	<li>Where are nightly backups stored?</li>
	<li>Who at the cloud provider has key access to encrypted data?</li>
</ol>
<p>
	If the answer to (1) is anywhere outside India, you are not buying ABDM-ready software. You are
	buying a brochure.
</p>
