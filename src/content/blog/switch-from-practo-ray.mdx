<h2 id="why">Why people switch</h2>
<p>
	Practo Ray works for solo practitioners. The friction starts when you add a second doctor, then a
	third — the schedule conflicts compound, the billing model strains, and the integrations you
	actually need (lab, pharmacy, GST e-invoicing) live in separate tools. Three of our six
	design-partner clinics were ex-Practo Ray. Here's how their migrations went.
</p>

<h2 id="day-0">Day 0 — Export</h2>
<p>
	Practo Ray's settings panel has a CSV export for appointments and patient master. Run it. Save
	both files. We'll need them on Day 1.
</p>

<h2 id="days-1-3">Days 1–3 — Import + validation</h2>
<ul>
	<li>
		Patient master goes in first. We dedupe by phone + DOB and report conflicts (typically 4-7% of
		records).
	</li>
	<li>
		Appointment history goes in next. Past appointments preserve who saw whom, when, and the visit
		type.
	</li>
	<li>
		Active appointments — the patient bookings still in the future — get rolled forward with
		confirmation messages.
	</li>
	<li>You run both systems in parallel. Lucoze in read-only mode, Practo Ray as primary.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="days-4-7">Days 4–7 — Switching billing</h2>
<p>
	Day 4: cutover billing to Lucoze. The front desk processes the day on Lucoze, with the old system
	open as backup. By day 7, 95% of staff stop opening the old tool.
</p>

<h2 id="days-8-14">Days 8–14 — Records consolidation</h2>
<p>
	Old clinical notes are read-only in Lucoze, alongside new notes. We don't try to OCR five years of
	free-text — we tag the visit dates and let your doctors browse when needed. New notes from Day 4
	forward are full-fidelity.
</p>

<h2 id="day-14">Day 14 — Practo Ray cancellation</h2>
<p>
	You export a final archive from Practo Ray (CSV + the PDF receipts they generate). You retain it
	for 7 years as required. You cancel the Practo Ray subscription. Done.
</p>

<h2 id="what">What you keep</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Every patient record, indexed and searchable in Lucoze.</li>
	<li>Every past appointment, with the right doctor and visit type.</li>
	<li>All active bookings, rolled forward.</li>
	<li>A full archive of the old system, for your own records.</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="cost">What this costs</h2>
<p>
	The migration is included on the Clinic Pro plan. Hospital plan adds a named implementation
	manager. There is no “migration fee” — it would be obscene to charge for moving your own data.
</p>
